Fast Weight Attention for Continual Learning

Overview

Fast Weight Attention studies recurrent memory updates as online learning rules under read-after-write autoregressive semantics. It introduces the Falcon family of normalized updates, spanning scalar, per-channel, and sliding-window variants, and derives recurrent, masked-parallel, and chunk-parallel implementations. The proposed methods remain competitive in language modeling while improving length extrapolation on variable-digit addition.
